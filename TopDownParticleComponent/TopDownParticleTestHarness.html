Room effects

Three room effect slots can each choose rain, snowfall, hail, ashfall, cinderfall, drifting motes, organic motes, or one of four falling-leaf recipes. Fog and haze will use a separate area-effect path.

Choose oak, maple, or oval leaves, or mix all three shapes together. Drifting Motes covers dust and magic; Organic Motes covers pollen and spores.

For falling styles, this is the exact time from spawn until the fall animation ends.

Set nonzero wind to bring leaves in from the upwind edge. They cross the room and leave at the downwind edge; travel time follows room size and wind speed.

Stationary surface marks

Place translucent stains on the room image. Click the room to position the selected mark. Each stain gets stable shape variation from its ID. Marks stay still until removed or their optional lifetime expires.
