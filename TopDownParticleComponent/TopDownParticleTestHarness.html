Advanced fire streams

Each stream starts from its recipe defaults. A field you change here overrides that field for this stream; simple Fire controls remain the fallback for fields left untouched. Reset clears this stream's advanced overrides, returning it to recipe defaults plus any shared Fire controls. Settings are saved per fire and per style. Smoke stays in its separate panel.

Live overrides: rate, lifetime range, size, spread, speed, start/end color and opacity, color timing, planar velocity offsets, alpha scale, and lighting pass. Texture, blend mode, draw order, spawn shape, and authored motion ranges require rebuilding the technical recipe.
